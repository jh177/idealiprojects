Submissions

Effective date: May 5, 2026

Ideali Projects does not accept or consider unsolicited creative submissions (including scripts, treatments, pitches, concepts, formats, characters, storylines, or other materials).

Do not send unsolicited materials

Please do not send unsolicited submissions through email, mail, social media, or any other channel. We only consider submissions at our request and typically through an authorized representative (e.g., agent/manager/attorney).

No confidential relationship

No confidential relationship is created by sending us materials, and we do not agree to treat unsolicited submissions as confidential.

If you still send materials

If you nonetheless send us unsolicited materials, you understand and agree that:

  • Your submission will be treated as non-confidential.
  • We have no obligation to review, acknowledge, or return the materials.
  • We may already be developing or may later develop projects similar to your submission.
  • You will not be entitled to compensation, credit, or any rights based on your submission.

Submission releases

If we ever request a submission, we may require a written submission release (or similar agreement) before reviewing any materials.
